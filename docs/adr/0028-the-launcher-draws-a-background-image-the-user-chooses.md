# The launcher draws a background image the user chooses

Accepted 2026-10-06 by the user's decision, after judging the look in the HTML background mockup (`docs/research/background-mockup`, the "Hero" preset). It lifts, for this one case, the rule that no wallpaper is painted inside Pane (`crates/pane/src/ui/material.rs`, `docs/research/ui-port/README.md`): that rule stands for the desktop's wallpaper, whose copy would fake translucency, not for a picture the user picks for the launcher.

The user chooses a picture on Settings' Appearance page, with the platform's file picker, and one of five background effects — None, Dither, ASCII, Halftone or Scanlines (the default) — Roboco's new-thread background treatments. Pane decodes the picture, keeps its own copy (at most 1920 on its longer side, a JPEG) in the data folder's `backgrounds` folder, and records only the copy's file name: `"background": "background-<time>.jpg"` and `"backgroundEffect": "scanlines"` in `settings.json`. A record naming anything but a plain file name fails to read, so nothing outside that folder is ever drawn. A copy the record no longer names is removed once the record is written. Removing the background brings the plain panel back.

Only the launcher draws it, behind everything it shows; the Settings window does not. The picture fills the panel's top, cover-fitted, its effect drawn into it, sharp at the top and blurred below the middle, and fades into the canvas by the panel's lower edge under a dim of the canvas. The canvas is the panel's own color moved 55% toward the picture's dominant color and held to a dark panel's lightness (6–16%) and at most 45% saturation, so the palette's text keeps its contrast. On the solid material the panel is the opaque canvas. On glass it is the canvas at the glass tint's alpha, the frame's alpha follows its fade and the picture is drawn at 84%, as Roboco's frosted new-thread background is, so the window's frost shows through the picture and the panel below it (amended 2026-10-06: the first version painted the opaque canvas on glass too, which hid the frost). As the results scroll the picture moves up 1.25 times as fast and has dissolved by the time they have scrolled half the panel. The search field becomes a frosted pill inside its 64px row (the compact window keeps its height), and the pins, the selected row and the footer are frosted too: a 30px blur of what is behind them inside the window under the canvas at 40% (the footer 60%) and a cool silver edge. Section labels and secondary text step up a shade over the picture. These values are fixed: the mockup's per-picture "auto contrast" was not chosen.

GPUI CE draws images but has no image masks, so the picture, its effect, the blur, the fades and the dim are composited on the CPU, off the window's thread, into one frame at the panel's size in physical pixels, baked again only when the picture, the effect, the palette, the scale factor or the material changes; the launcher keeps the last frame until the next is ready.

Not done yet: Roboco's framing (pan and zoom), a folder of pictures to shuffle, a crossfade between pictures, a switch to keep the panel's own color, a tuned light palette, and turning the picture off where the system asks for less transparency or high contrast.
